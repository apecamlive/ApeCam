# Harmful stream

**You notice:** Telegram auto-blur alert (3 reports within 5 minutes, D9), a severe-category report, or a
user message.

## Steps (moderator)

1. Admin → Reports or Live streams. Open the stream (it is already blurred for viewers if auto-blur fired).
2. Decide:
   - **Kill** the stream: ends it for everyone immediately (server-side, the streamer cannot resume it).
   - **Ban** the wallet: 24 h, 7 days or permanent. A ban also signs the user out everywhere and ends their
     live streams. Banned users cannot sign in, start streams, chat or report.
   - **Hide** the token if the token room itself is the problem (scam token, impersonation).
   - **Dismiss** reports that are wrong; this lifts auto-blur.
3. Every action is in Admin → Action log with who, when and why.

## Illegal content (e.g. anything involving minors)

1. Kill the stream and permanently ban the wallet **first**.
2. Do not download or share the content. Snapshots are in the R2 public bucket under the stream id; ask the
   owner (not a moderator) to preserve them if a report to authorities is needed, then delete them.
3. The owner reports to the relevant authority / hotline for the jurisdiction.
